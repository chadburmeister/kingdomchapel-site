Mission · Vision · Aims · Objectives

That all the nations of the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the Lord.

Habakkuk 2:14

Mission

Liberty for humanity from captivity, for eternity. Matthew 28:19

Vision

That all the nations of the earth will be filled with knowledge of the word of the Lord, thereby bringing the people of God in the world to the Lordship of our Saviour Jesus Christ. Habakkuk 2:14

Purpose

  • Raise awareness of the kingdom of Jesus Christ through preaching of the word of God.
  • Depopulation of hell by winning souls at all costs.

Aims

  • Demonstrate the love of Jesus Christ to humanity.
  • Restore human identity and dignity.
  • Facilitate the release of detainees from jails.
  • Provide overall social services to the needy and vulnerable in deprived communities.
  • Provision of social care to the destitute.
  • Provision of welfare services for orphan children, especially those orphaned by Covid-19.
  • Provision of welfare services for the aged, between 75–85 years old.
  • Support the mentally impaired in their reformation and transformation.
  • Career and human capital development through skills and vocational training.
  • Provide support to people affected by natural disasters like earthquakes, erosion, and mudslides.

Objectives

  • People will experience the love of Christ.
  • The lost souls will accept Jesus as Lord and Saviour.
  • The people will discover their values and worth.
  • Humans will be hopeful and faithful to God.
  • Sufferings are abated and eradicated.
