Our Ministries

Faith in action, every day.

Kingdom Chapel International Ministries helps our members in many ways — feeding the elderly, feeding families in need, and helping young people find a better life after prison.

Expanding access to education

This year, we're expanding the ministry — God willing — to provide tuition for 3–4 of our brothers and sisters in Christ, so they can attend university and learn how to help our community and the people in it to prosper.

A $500 donation sends one student to university for an entire semester.
Our Projects

Where we're headed next.

These initiatives are in early stages of development as we grow our capacity in Freetown. Your support helps us build them out fully.

Materials for Learning

Providing books, supplies, and learning materials for children and students in our community who otherwise couldn't afford them.

Education for Teachers

Training and equipping local teachers with the resources they need to give children a stronger start in school.

Support for Communities

Practical, on-the-ground support for families and neighborhoods facing hardship — food, shelter, and care for the vulnerable.

Help us grow these ministries.

Every gift moves this work forward — for a child, a family, a community.
