Member Testimonies

Changed lives, one story at a time.

“For God so loved the world that he gave his only begotten Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish, but have everlasting life.” — John 3:16

Members of Kingdom Chapel International Ministries share the impact of the living God and Jesus Christ on their lives — from struggle and addiction to renewed purpose, healing, and hope.

“If you save me from this situation, I will give myself to you, I will stay away from this wayward life and stubborn attitude.”

Before accepting Christ, this member was stubborn and would go to clubs, smoke, and drink. She came to fully believe in God after facing a brutal accident.

Before accepting Jesus, this man says he was possessed. When he gave his life to Jesus, God be the glory — his ways, and even the way he dresses, have changed.

Before accepting Christ, this man was involved in a different religion and was a perpetual sinner, always chasing pleasures. To God be the glory — since accepting Christ, he has changed his ways.

“Before accepting Christ as my Lord and personal saviour, I was living a sinful life and things were not working. I can say that it was all by God's grace — a friend invited me, and when I came to church, the spirit of God took over my life. Living the life of Christ is a life of light.”

“I was having a terrible sickness for over ten years, crying behind closed doors. When I came to Christ, the sickness went away.”

“The Council for Humanitarian Empowerment and Natural Disasters gives us the word of God. Teaching us the word of God opens up our eyes, and empowers us every day. And we take care of all of our basic needs.”
