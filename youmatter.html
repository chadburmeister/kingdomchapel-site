Discovering purpose as one of the most effective remedies for depression and hopelessness.

▶ Watch Darryll Stinson's TEDx Talk

In this heartfelt talk, author and suicide survivor Darryll Stinson shares three tips to help viewers discover their highest purpose, in order to cope with depression and live a more fulfilled life. According to the World Health Organization, an estimated 264 million people of all ages around the world suffer from depression.

Darryll wrestles with tough questions like: why do some of the most famous and wealthy people in the world take their own lives? Why do people feel so lonely when technology and social media make it so easy to stay connected?

Darryll, a former Division I athlete and founder of Second Chance Athletes, presents the idea that understanding one's unique purpose in life can serve as one of the most effective remedies for depression. He's been featured on FOX, ABC, NCAA, TEDx, and some of the top podcasts in the United States. After going from suicidal to successful, Darryll now helps athletes and entrepreneurs shed their past stories and create a massive impact that aligns with their highest purpose.

Darryll is the author of the Amazon best-selling book Who Am I After Sports? An Athlete's Roadmap to Discover New Purpose and Live Fulfilled. When he's not working, he enjoys spending time with his wife and three daughters.

This talk was given at a TEDx event using the TED conference format but independently organized by a local community. Learn more at ted.com/tedx.
