Make an impact on eternity.

Thank you for joining the Kingdom Chapel International Ministries team by donating today. Investing in just one child's release from prison, for $625, has the potential to impact entire cities in Africa. A $100 donation feeds up to 10 families for an entire day.

Apostle Dr. Moses Simon
Leader, Kingdom Chapel International Ministries

“Liberation of humanity from captivity for eternity.” — Matthew 28:19

Apostle Dr. Moses Simon founded Kingdom Chapel International Ministries in Freetown, Sierra Leone, to preach the gospel and meet the practical needs of vulnerable children and families — feeding the hungry, bailing children out of prison, and walking alongside the community in faith.

Supporting Partner

Chad Burmeister

Co-Founder & CEO, Living A Better Story, Inc.

Chad met Apostle Dr. Moses Simon after recording an episode of the Living A Better Story podcast, and has since committed to supporting the ministry's work — connecting donors, sharing updates, and helping build a bridge to Christ through the Living A Better Story podcast and the 77Pray app.
