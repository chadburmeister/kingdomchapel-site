A Reflection

If God is Love, and Love is God…

…then listen to what happens when you replace the word “Love” with “God” in John Denver's best-loved song.

John Denver wrote “Perhaps Love” while grieving the loss of his father. In that low place, he remembered how many people were there for him, and found himself doing exactly what he was meant to do. As he put it, the words “came to him out of thin air” — what happens, he said, when you talk to God and listen.

“This song I think is the best I've ever written. I was feeling very lost, and what happened is that I started thinking about you people, and how lucky I am to do the things I love. It is your song, and it was written for each of you — it's called Perhaps Love.” — John Denver, Singer, Songwriter, Environmental Activist

We invite you to listen to “Perhaps Love” below, and as you do, try quietly substituting the word God wherever you hear the word Love.
