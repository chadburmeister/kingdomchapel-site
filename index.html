Our Why

Saving one child changes a village.

“A great thing about saving a child for Jesus is that one child can influence a whole family and friends, and in many cases an entire village for Jesus.” — Apostle Dr. Moses Simon, Leader, Kingdom Chapel International Ministries

Some of Africa's prisons are considered the worst on earth — truly “hell on earth.” When a young person hits rock bottom, hungry and desperate, they can end up behind bars for nothing more than trying to survive. Kingdom Chapel steps in: we post bail, minister to the child and their family, and walk with them toward a new life in Christ.
